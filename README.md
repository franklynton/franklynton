## Featured projects

**[Millennium Bug](https://github.com/franklynton/Millenium-Bug)** · TypeScript · [▶ Play it live](https://millenium-bug-ashen.vercel.app)

An AI virtual pet built in 24 hours at HackNotts '26 with a team of four. Players raise a
"millennium bug" that survived Y2K: it learns from how it's treated, grows up through four
life stages, and glitches when neglected. Built with Next.js, TypeScript and Supabase on
Vercel, with LLM chat, a real-time multiplayer lobby, mini-games and a pixel-art editor.
I led development and brought the team's work together into one live product.

**[Inventory-Contingent Market Maker](https://github.com/franklynton/inventory-market-maker)** · Python

A market-making simulator that quotes bid-offer prices on five equities in response to
simulated hedge fund requests. Quotes are skewed asymmetrically across five inventory
tiers, widening and shifting prices as a position builds to discourage further
accumulation. Every trade and position is logged, and cumulative P&L and spread
dynamics are plotted for each asset.

**[Portfolio Risk Analytics Pipeline](https://github.com/franklynton/portfolio-risk-analytics)** · Python

A pipeline that computes daily and cumulative returns, volatility, rolling beta and
Sharpe ratio for a multi-asset portfolio, and measures tail risk through historical
VaR, CVaR and maximum drawdown, with Matplotlib charts that track risk over time.
Unit-tested with pytest.

**[Equity Portfolio Construction & Optimisation](https://github.com/franklynton/equity-portfolio-optimisation)** · Python

A 10-stock portfolio across five sectors, with daily and average returns for each
stock and portfolio return and variance derived from the full variance-covariance
matrix, starting from equal weights. Mean-variance optimisation then finds the
minimum-variance and maximum-Sharpe portfolios on the efficient frontier.
